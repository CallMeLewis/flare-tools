# Name and icon

The code in this repository is free software under the GNU General Public License v3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The
licence covers the code. It does not cover the name **Flare Tools** or its icon, which identify the official app made
by CallMeLewis.

## What you can do

- Build and run the app from this code, and change it for your own use.
- Share copies of the official, unmodified app under its own name.
- Say that your project is based on Flare Tools, or works with it, in plain words.

## What needs a different name

If you share a modified version, give it a name and icon of its own, so nobody mistakes it for the official app. Don't
use "Flare Tools", its icon or anything confusingly similar for it, and don't present it as official or endorsed.

This is in line with section 7(e) of the GPL, which lets a licensor decline to grant rights to their names and marks.
